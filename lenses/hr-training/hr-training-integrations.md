# HR & Training Lens — Integrations

> **Status: Stub** — Integration points identified. Content to be developed by the working group.

---

## Expected Coverage

- Alignment with competency frameworks (SHRM, DDI, CEB/Gartner) and where C4E-specific competencies extend or diverge from them
- Performance management system design: connecting values-based criteria to HRIS platforms (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, etc.) without losing behavioral nuance
- Learning management systems and how to instrument behavior change measurement beyond completion rates
- Job architecture and leveling frameworks: where Domain Translator and Convergence Facilitator roles fit in standard grade structures
- Compensation philosophy integration: how cross-domain contribution is weighted in total rewards models
- Organizational network analysis (ONA) as a measurement tool for cross-domain collaboration patterns — a behavioral signal that complements self-reported review data
- Connection to change management frameworks (Prosci ADKAR, Kotter) for the transition between maturity levels, which are fundamentally people transitions
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion considerations: convergence competency definitions must be audited for cultural and socioeconomic bias in how they're assessed
